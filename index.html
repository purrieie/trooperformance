FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the audit check?

Three things. Speed: Google's PageSpeed lab test plus real-shopper Chrome data, on mobile and desktop. Search: indexing, titles, meta descriptions, canonicals, headings, alt text, sitemap and product schema. AI answers: which AI crawlers your robots.txt allows, llms.txt, and how much of your page is readable without JavaScript.

Is it free?

Yes. No login and nothing to install. You get your scores, the issues behind them and a link you can share with your developer.

What is AEO?

AEO (answer engine optimisation) means making your store easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini to read, understand and recommend. It starts with letting their crawlers in and giving them clean, structured facts about your products.

How are the Search and AI scores worked out?

Each check counts 1 for a pass, 0.5 for a warning and 0 for a fail. The score is the average across the checks that apply to your page, out of 100. The speed score is Google's own Lighthouse performance score.
